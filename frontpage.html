U.S. jets hit Iran’s Qeshm Island as Tehran’s drones strike Bahrain and Kuwait

American warplanes struck a Revolutionary Guard missile site on Qeshm Island, at the mouth of the Strait of Hormuz, after Iran fired drones at U.S. bases in the Gulf. Iran answered across the water: drones aimed at Ali Al Salem air base in Kuwait and a U.S. radar site in Bahrain were intercepted, with no casualties reported.
